title: Android widget
description: A Home Screen widget through the small Coinio Widget app.
---

The widget shows what is left to pay this month and your next payments, like the [iPhone widget](/docs/integrations/iphone-widget). On Android it comes as a small open-source app, **Coinio Widget**, whose only job is the widget. It is distributed as an APK on the [GitHub releases](https://github.com/yakupemreyerli/coinio/releases/latest), not on Google Play.

Your phone has to reach Coinio wherever it is, so you need an [https address](/docs/expose). A LAN address only works while you are at home.

## Set it up

<Steps>
<Step>

On your phone, download `coinio-widget-<version>.apk` from the [latest release](https://github.com/yakupemreyerli/coinio/releases/latest) and open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser.

</Step>
<Step>

In Coinio, open **Settings › Integrations › Android widget** and tap **Connect Android**. Coinio makes a read-only key and shows a QR code. Open Coinio Widget and tap **Scan the QR code**, or, if you are looking at Coinio on the same phone, tap **Open in the app**.

</Step>
<Step>

Long-press the Home Screen, tap **Widgets** and add **Coinio**. Resize it for more rows.

</Step>
</Steps>

## How it behaves

- Three sizes: small shows the total and the next payment, medium the next four, large the next six with "in 3 days" under each. Overdue payments are red.
- It refreshes about every 30 minutes while there is a network, and right after you connect. Opening the app refreshes it too.
- Without a connection it shows the last data it saw and marks it *offline* with the time of that data.
- If the key is deleted in Coinio, it says *API key rejected*; connect again from Settings.
- Tapping the widget opens the app, which shows the same list and an **Open Coinio** button.

## Privacy

The app talks only to your Coinio, and only to `/api/widget` with the read-only key: it can see your upcoming payments but cannot change anything. The key is stored encrypted on the phone. **Disconnect** in the app forgets it; delete the key under **Settings › Integrations › API keys** to revoke it everywhere.

## Updates

New versions come with each Coinio release. [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium) can watch the GitHub releases and update the app for you: add `https://github.com/yakupemreyerli/coinio` and keep only files matching `coinio-widget`.
